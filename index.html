SPARAGMOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

SPARAGMOS is a game by Goiaba Lunar.

We collect no personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no online features. It does not connect to the internet.

The only things the game stores are your settings and your progress (for example, volume, control style and your current ASCENT run). They are saved on your own device, are never sent anywhere, and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

The game asks for one permission: vibration, so your phone can buzz when a blow lands. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to share with or sell to anyone.

If this ever changes (for example, if online features are added), this policy will be updated first.

Questions: write to Goiaba Lunar at gscdevelop@outlook.com.
